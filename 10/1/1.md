This chart shows how energy is used in an average Australian household energy use and greenhouse gas emissions.

Overall, the highest catogory in energy use is heating, meanwhile water heating obtains the highest percentages in gas emissions. cooling on the other hand, holds the least percentages among the 2 pie charts. The percentages for lighting and refrigeration doubles for its gas emissions. Other appliances in energy use appears to be more than doubled for gas emissions.

For energy use, heating takes the biggest percentage at around 42%, while water heating takes around 30%. Other appliances has about 15%, followed by refrigeration and lighting, which are both around 4%. Cooling have the lowest percentage, at only about 2%. This shows that heating is used much more than the other categories.

For gas emissions, water heating has the highest percentage, at around 32%. Other appliances comes second, at about 28%, while heating is around 15%. Lighting and refrigeration have both increased compared with their energy use percentages. Cooling still has the lowest percentage at around 3%. Overall, some categories has produced more gas emissions than their percentage of energy use.
