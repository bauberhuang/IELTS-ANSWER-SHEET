In recent years, whether countries are becoming more similar is a positive development or not has become a debated topic. This essay will look at both views before giving my opinion. In my opinion, countries becoming more similar can be useful, but it can also cause problems.

Those who support this idea argue that it can make people's lives easier. For example, people can find the same shops and restaurants in different countries, so they feel more comfortable when they travel because they already know these shops and products. Growing similarity can also help people understand each other, because shared brands and trends give them something in common to talk about.

On the other hand, others believe that this is a bad thing, mainly because traditional cultures can become less important. For example, young people may prefer foreign food, music, and clothes instead of traditional things from their own country, and over time this can weaken their connection to certain customs. This can be a problem because culture is closely tied to a community's identity, and once traditions are lost, they are hard to bring back.

Admittedly, some people may say that becoming more similar is important because it makes travelling easier. However, this convenience is temporary, whereas losing a unique culture affects a whole community permanently. So, I think countries can have some similar things, but they should still protect their own culture.

In conclusion, countries becoming more similar can make people's lives easier, but I believe this should not come at the cost of traditional culture, which countries should continue to protect.
