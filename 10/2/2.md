In recent years, whether countries are being more similar every day is a positive fact or not. This essay will look at both views before giving my own opinion. In my opinion, countries becoming more similar can be useful, but it can also cause some problems.

Those who support countries becoming more similar argue that it can make people's lives easier. For example, people can find the same shops and restaurants in different countries. Then, they may feel more comfortable when they travel to another country. This is because they already know these shops and products. Countries becoming more similar can also help people understand each other. They may have similar lifestyles and interests. They may also find it easier to communicate with people from other countries.

On the other hand, others believe that countries becoming more similar is a bad thing. This is because traditional cultures may become less important. For example, young people may prefer foreign food, music and clothes instead of traditional things from their own country. This shows that people may slowly forget some parts of their culture. Also, some traditional things may not be passed to the next generation. This can be a problem because culture is important for every country.

Admittedly, some people may say that becoming more similar is important because it can make travelling and living in other countries easier. However, if countries become too similar, some special cultures may disappear. People may also stop caring about traditional things. This can make countries less different from each other. So, I think countries can have some similar things, but they should still protect their own culture.

In conclusion, I believe countries becoming more similar can be useful in some situations, because it can make people's lives easier and help people understand each other. However, countries should not become too similar because traditional cultures may become less important. I think having some similar things is good, but every country should keep its own culture.
