In recent years, whether countries are becoming more similar is a positive fact or not. This essay will look at both views before giving my opinion. In my opinion, countries becoming more similar can be useful, but it can also cause problems.

Those who support this idea argue that it can make people's lives easier. For example, people can find the same shops and restaurants in different countries. Then, they can feel more comfortable when they travel because they already know these shops and products. Countries becoming more similar can also helps people understand each other. They can have similar lifestyles and interests.

On the other hand, others believe that this is a bad thing. This is because traditional cultures can become less important. For example, young people may prefer foreign food, music, and clothes instead of traditional things from their own country. This show that people can slowly forget parts of their culture. Also, some traditional things cannot be passed to the next generation. This can be a problem because culture are important for every country.

Admittedly, some people may say that becoming more similar is important because it makes travelling easier. However, if countries become too similar, some special cultures can disappear. People can also stop caring about traditional things. So, I think countries can have some similar things, but they should still protects their own culture.

In conclusion, countries becoming more similar can make people's lives easier. However, countries should not become too similar because traditional cultures can become less important.
