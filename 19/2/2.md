Nowadays, some people believe that the working week should be shorter and workers should have a longer weekend. I agree because a shorter working week can give workers more time to rest and make them work better.

Firstly, a shorter working week can be good because they have more time to rest. If people work for many days, they can become tired. Therefore, they may not have enough energy for their work. As a result, companies may not get good results. Because of this, they can have more energy when they return to work. Therefore, they may focus better, and this can help them finish tasks faster and make fewer mistakes. So, a longer weekend can be useful for workers and companies. This can be better for companies too.

Secondly, a shorter working week can give people more time for their personal life. Therefore, they can feel more happy. If people are happier, they can have a better attitude towards their work. This can help them work better.

Admittedly, some people may say companies need workers to work longer because they have a lot of work to do. This is understandable because companies need finish their work on time. If workers work fewer days, some work may take longer. However, I think it is OK because workers can be more rested and focused. As a result, they can finish their work better even if they work fewer days.

In conclusion, I agree that the working week should be shorter because workers can rest more and spend time on their personal life. Therefore, they can feel better and work more effectively.
