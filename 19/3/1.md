The diagram shows how ethanol, a biofuel, is made from plants. There are several stages in this process, start from plants growing and ending with ethanol being used in vehicles.

Overall, the process has many steps, and it needs sunlight and carbon dioxide at the beginning. First, plants grow, then they are processed into ethanol, and finally the ethanol is use for cars, trucks, and airplanes, which produce carbon dioxide.

First, energy from sunlight and carbon dioxide help plants and trees to grow. After that, the plants are harvested by machines. Next, the plants go to pre-processing, and this makes cellulose. The cellulose then goes to a processing factory.

In the factory, the cellulose is changed into sugars. After microbes are added to the sugars, ethanol is produced. The ethanol is put into cars, trucks, and airplanes to use as fuel. When these vehicles are driven, they release a lot of carbon dioxide into the air. This carbon dioxide can be used by plants again, so the process can start again.
